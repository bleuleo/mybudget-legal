Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 July 2026  ·  Last Updated: 21 November 2026

Privacy-first by design. MyBudget2U is designed so that your financial information remains under your control. Your budget — entries, accounts, budgets and receipts — is stored on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. The exceptions are a payment notification from an app you chose (section 7) and the words of a screenshot you share (section 1): only after you agree in the app, they are sent to our server to be read and are not kept there. Optional cloud backup uses your own Google Drive account.

1 Information you provide

You enter this information into the app:

TransactionsAccountsBudgetsCategoriesReceipt imagesNotesProfile nameProfile picture

This information remains on your device unless you choose to back it up to Google Drive.

When you scan a receipt with the camera, its text is read on your device. When you share a receipt or payment screenshot to MyBudget2U, the app first reads its text on your device. From app version 1.3.12, and only if you choose Allow when the app asks (you can change this any time in Settings → Auto-capture), only the words the app read from the screenshot are then sent to our server to find the payment; the picture itself is never sent. Before sending, the app leaves out lines that look like a home address or hold a phone number (such as a delivery contact) (except that any money amounts on such a line are still sent), email addresses and your profile name, and replaces long ID, order, account, card and identity-card (IC) numbers, including ones split by spaces or dashes, with placeholders. This includes screenshots of a move between your own accounts or wallets and of amounts in another currency; the app still decides on your phone whether a payment is a move between your own accounts, and in which currency. Amounts, dates, shop names and the other words on the screen are sent as they are, together with your currency setting, what the app itself read (the amount, and whether money went out or came in) and the same anonymous identifier described in section 4. Our server passes the words to Anthropic’s Claude, the service provider described in sections 7 and 8, which says whether the screen shows a payment and its amount, date, shop and currency; the app checks the answer against the screen before using it. So that only the genuine app can ask for a reading, the app also asks Google Play for an integrity check (Google’s Play Integrity API) and sends its result with the request, as described in section 7. Our server does not store or log the words. It keeps only counts (plus the one-way codes described under “Corrections” below): how many screenshots each anonymous identifier has had read this month, and daily totals by kind of app, with no text. The one exception is the developer’s own test phones: for those, and only those, the words sent, what the app itself read and the answer are kept for 14 days to fix reading mistakes, then deleted. If you choose Keep on my phone, have no connection, or the server can’t read it, the screenshot is read on your device only.

Corrections. If, within 7 days, you save an entry the server helped read (from a notification or a screenshot) with a different amount or type than the server read, or change its amount or type later, delete or undo it, correct or discard it on the check sheet, or tap Ignore on a payment pop-up or To-review alert, the app later tells the server in the background, only while server reading for that kind is still allowed. It sends the same anonymous identifier and Google Play integrity check used for readings, which kind of reading it was, whether the amount or type changed or the entry was deleted or ignored, and whether the new amount appears on what was read (yes or no) — never the amount, the name, the text or the entry. The server keeps daily counts of these for up to 90 days. For corrections where the new amount appears on the screen, and for payments you ignore (on a pop-up or in To review), it also keeps for 30 days a one-way code made from your identifier and the kind of reading (not the identifier itself), with the day the install was first used and the time of the latest such report. For 7 days after each reading it keeps a one-way code of which kind of reading was given to your install, so that corrections only count for real readings.

2 Where your data is stored

Everything listed above is stored locally on your device in the app’s private database. We, the developer, have no server that receives or stores this data, and we cannot see it. (Sections 1 and 7 describe the screenshot words and payment notifications that, with your agreement, are read on our server.)

3 Google Drive backup

Receipt photos are stored locally on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup, they — together with your database — are included in a backup stored privately in your own Google Drive account, where Google encrypts it at rest. The backup lives in a private application folder that only the app can see; only you can access it. You can delete these backups from your Drive at any time.

4 Subscriptions & payments

To offer and manage the Pro subscription we use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. When you purchase or restore Pro, these services process your purchase and a randomly-generated anonymous identifier to determine whether your subscription is active. We never receive your card or payment details — Google Play handles all payment.

If you redeem a promo code, the code and that same anonymous identifier are sent to our own small server, hosted on Cloudflare. It checks the code, keeps a record of which identifier claimed it, and asks RevenueCat to give you the free month. Cloudflare also handles your IP address while the request is made, as any web host does.

5 Currency exchange rates

When you record spending in a foreign currency, the app fetches current rates from a third-party rate service. Only a request for rates is sent — no personal or financial data leaves your device.

6 Notifications

Budget alerts and daily reminders are generated locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

7 Payment notifications (optional)

Payment notifications are off unless you turn them on. When you do, MyBudget2U uses Android’s notification access to read notifications only from the banking and wallet apps you choose, looking for a payment’s amount, the shop or person, and the time, so it can offer to log it. Android’s permission screen says the app can read all your notifications, because Android has no narrower permission; MyBudget2U ignores every app you have not chosen.

  • What is kept: only notifications that contain a money amount. Login codes, messages and everything else are discarded immediately and never stored. One that turns out not to be a payment, such as an offer that only names a price, is deleted once it has been read.
  • Where, and for how long: on your device only, in the app’s private storage, for at most 7 days, or until you log or ignore the payment.
  • Sent to our server to be read, only after you agree: from app version 1.3.10, when you turn on payment notifications the app asks you on screen to agree that alerts from the apps you choose are sent to MyBudget2U’s server to be read. If you decline, nothing is sent and the app reads alerts on your phone only. If you agree, a notification that contains a money amount — including an offer that only names a price — from a banking or wallet app you chose, is sent as its title and text, exactly as your bank or wallet wrote them, to our own server, hosted on Cloudflare, together with the app’s name, the time it arrived, your currency setting and the same anonymous identifier described in section 4. The text can include names (yours, or the other person’s) and the last digits of an account or card. Our server passes it to Anthropic’s Claude, an AI model, which says whether it is a payment, its amount, direction and shop or person. The app checks the answer before using it. Nothing from your budget is sent: no entries, accounts, categories, notes or profile. Notifications with no money amount, and login or transfer codes, never leave your phone. From app version 1.3.12, this includes a notification about a move between your own accounts or wallets, such as a top-up, and one with an amount in another currency; the app still decides on your phone whether a payment is a move between your own accounts, and in which currency. Earlier versions read those on your phone only. If the server can’t be reached, or you have used your free readings, the app falls back to reading on your phone.
  • Not kept: our server processes the notification text in memory and does not store or log it. It keeps counts (and, for corrections you make, the records described under “Corrections” in section 1): how many notifications each anonymous identifier has had read, and daily totals used to prevent abuse. To recognise known wordings, it may also keep the wording pattern of a notification that was read, with amounts, names, numbers, account numbers and reference codes replaced by placeholders (for example “You have paid {AMOUNT} to {PERSON}.”), together with the name of the banking or wallet app and a count. A pattern holds no names, amounts, account numbers or identifiers, is never linked to you or your identifier, and rare ones are deleted after 30 days. The one exception is the developer’s own test phones: for the anonymous identifiers of phones the developer owns, and only for those, the full notification text is kept for 14 days to find and fix reading mistakes, then deleted. Anthropic processes the text to answer and handles it under its commercial terms and privacy policy. So that only the genuine app can ask for a reading, the app also asks Google Play for an integrity check (Google’s Play Integrity API) and sends its result with the request; Google processes the device and app signals this needs under its own terms. This only applies if you turned on “Read payment notifications” and agreed to server reading (from app version 1.3.12, agreed again to the updated wording); earlier versions (1.3.5 onward) send a notification only when the phone can’t read it.
  • Never backed up: the notification text is never included in your exports or Google Drive backups. A payment you log becomes an ordinary entry, and the list of apps you chose is saved with your settings; both are kept like the rest of your data (sections 2 and 3).
  • Turning it off: switch off “Read payment notifications” under Payment alerts in Settings → Auto-capture, which also deletes any payments still waiting, or remove MyBudget2U’s access in Android Settings → Notification access.

8 Third-party services

Google Sign-In, Google Drive Backup, and Google Play Billing are governed by Google’s applicable terms and privacy policies. RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Anthropic (which reads the payment notifications described in section 7 and the screenshot words described in section 1) are governed by their own privacy policies. They act as our service providers. We encourage you to review them.

9 What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data. The service providers in section 8 process only what is needed for the features you use.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or trackers on your financial data. (RevenueCat processes only the subscription information described above.)

10 Keeping & deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Export a full JSON backup any time (Settings → Data).
  • Delete individual items in the app.
  • Clear the app’s data or uninstall to remove everything from the device.
  • Delete Drive backups from your Google Drive.

11 Children

MyBudget2U is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

12 Security

Your data is held in the app’s private storage on your device and, if you enable backup, in your private Google Drive folder. Please protect your device with a screen lock.
